> Meet Laser Tech Service partners — salons, aesthetic clinics and specialists whose work and equipment we know and recommend to our customers.

At Laser Tech Service, we do more than repair and maintain equipment. Over years of working together, we have come to know many salons, clinics and specialists whose equipment we service regularly.

This page introduces partners we confidently recommend to our customers.

We know the equipment they use because we maintain and repair it. This gives us an understanding of its technical condition and the importance its owners place on treatment quality and safety.

> Laser Tech Service recommends businesses whose work and equipment we know first-hand.
