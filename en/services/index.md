## Frequently asked questions

### What equipment do you service?

We service diode lasers, IPL, Nd:YAG, CO₂ and picosecond lasers, RF, LPG and cryotherapy devices, and other aesthetic equipment.

### Do you repair lasers from different manufacturers?

Yes. We work with equipment from many manufacturers. We confirm whether a particular model can be repaired after receiving its details and a description of the fault.

### How long does an equipment repair take?

Most standard repairs are completed within 1–3 working days. The time required depends on the fault and the availability of parts.

### Do you offer on-site repairs?

Yes. Depending on the equipment and fault, diagnostics or repairs can be carried out at the client's premises.

### Can I send equipment by courier?

Yes. Please contact us before sending equipment to our service centre. It must be properly protected for transport.

### Do you provide a warranty on the repair?

Yes. We provide a warranty on the servicing work. Its duration depends on the repair and the parts used.
