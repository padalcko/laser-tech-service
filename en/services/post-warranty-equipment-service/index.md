## Service scope

### Diagnostics after warranty expiry

We check technical condition, identify faults and assess repairability. We consider design, symptom history and component availability.

### Electronics and mechanical repair

We repair power supplies, boards, cables and mechanisms where component condition and design allow. Scope follows diagnostics.

### Replacing faulty parts

We select compatible components, replace worn assemblies and check operation after repair. We do not assume parts are available for every model.

### Preventive maintenance

We clean the device and check cooling, electronics, mechanisms and operating parameters. Preventive work is agreed according to device condition.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does post-warranty servicing mean?

Diagnostics, repair and maintenance after the manufacturer's or seller's warranty expires, with scope agreed for the specific device.

### Do I need a new device when the warranty expires?

No. You can first assess its condition, repair feasibility and whether repair is economically justified.

### Why is there no single starting price?

Cost depends on diagnostics, damage, required parts and scope. We quote individually.

### Can you source parts for every model?

We do not claim availability of all parts. We check whether compatible components can be obtained before agreeing the repair.

### Can I order preventive maintenance only?

Yes, the scope may include condition assessment, cleaning and system checks without a previously reported breakdown.

### When is the repair decision made?

After diagnostics, we discuss faults, repair feasibility and cost. The final scope and parts are approved with the customer before work.
