Post-warranty aesthetic equipment servicing

Technical support, diagnostics, repair and preventive maintenance after the manufacturer's or seller's warranty expires. The end of a warranty does not mean a new device is needed: we assess condition and the feasibility of an economically justified repair.

Quoted individually after diagnostics

Diagnostics are free when you commission repair or maintenance from Laser Tech Service.

Engineer travel and transport costs are charged separately.

Service scope

Diagnostics after warranty expiry

We check technical condition, identify faults and assess repairability. We consider design, symptom history and component availability.

Electronics and mechanical repair

We repair power supplies, boards, cables and mechanisms where component condition and design allow. Scope follows diagnostics.

Replacing faulty parts

We select compatible components, replace worn assemblies and check operation after repair. We do not assume parts are available for every model.

Preventive maintenance

We clean the device and check cooling, electronics, mechanisms and operating parameters. Preventive work is agreed according to device condition.

When to arrange a service

Manufacturer's or seller's warranty has expired

A fault in a device used for many years

Need to assess whether repair is worthwhile

Worn parts and connections

No up-to-date service inspection

Uncertainty about technical condition

Diagnostic and service stages

  1. Report and identify the device

    Provide the model, location and symptoms so we can determine the initial inspection scope.

  2. Technical inspection

    We check technical condition, identify faults and assess repairability. We consider design, symptom history and component availability.

  3. Agree the solution

    Cost is assessed individually according to the fault, repairability and required parts. Final work and replacement parts are agreed with the customer.

  4. Post-service checks

    Post-repair operational assessment and discussion of ongoing maintenance.
