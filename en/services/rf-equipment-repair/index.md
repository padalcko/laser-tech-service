## Service scope

### RF generator and control electronics repair

Diagnostics of the generator, power components and controls. Circuit board repair and operational checks after the work.

### RF handpiece and applicator repair

Checks of cables, connectors and contacts. Replacement of damaged connections and compatible handpiece components.

### RF microneedling mechanism repair

In microneedling models, we check needle extension, drives, sensors and control electronics. We test the mechanism after repair. We repair microneedling mechanism controls.

### RF parameter checks and calibration

We check generator stability and measure output using an appropriate instrument. Adjustment or calibration is performed where provided for by the model's manufacturer. We diagnose deviations in radiofrequency parameters.

## When diagnostics are needed

We establish the cause by inspecting the specific device.

### No RF energy

The handpiece does not heat or the generator operates unstably.

### Errors and shutdowns

The device reports an error or shuts down during use.

### Damaged connections

The handpiece cable or connector is damaged.

### Needles do not extend

The problem concerns the mechanism in a microneedling device.

## How the repair works

1. ### Service request

Describe the symptoms and provide the device model and location.
2. ### Diagnostics

We inspect components associated with the symptoms and identify the cause.
3. ### Agreement

Before repair, we agree the scope, parts availability and cost.
4. ### Repair and checks

We carry out the agreed work and check operation of the repaired components.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you repair RF microneedling equipment?

Yes, diagnostics may include the needle extension mechanism, drives, sensors and controls. We confirm repair feasibility for the model.

### Does a lack of heat mean the generator is faulty?

Not always. The cause may be the handpiece, connections, controls or generator. Diagnostics are needed.

### Can you repair an applicator cable?

We check the cable, connectors and contacts, then determine whether repair or replacement with a compatible part is possible.

### Is calibration possible for every RF device?

No. It depends on the design, manufacturer documentation and available adjustment and measurement methods.

### How much does servicing cost?

Prices start at EUR 165. Parts and additional work are quoted after diagnostics.

### When are diagnostics free?

When you commission repair or maintenance. Travel is charged separately.

### Do you service equipment outside Warsaw?

We handle requests from throughout Poland. Travel arrangements and costs are agreed individually.

### What information should I provide?

The device model, symptoms, displayed error and location. Photographs of a damaged cable or connector are helpful.
