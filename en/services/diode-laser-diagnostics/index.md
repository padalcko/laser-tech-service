## When does a diode laser need diagnostics?

Similar symptoms can have different causes. The cause can only be established by examining the specific device. We tailor the work to the model and problem; not every diagnostic assessment requires the same measurements, dismantling or calibration.

### The laser will not switch on

The problem may involve the incoming power supply, connections, power supply unit or control system. A device that does not respond does not necessarily have a damaged laser module.

### Handpiece not cooling

The cause may involve coolant circulation, heat dissipation, power or cooling control. In models with thermoelectric cooling, the Peltier module may also be relevant.

### The laser has lost output

Reduced performance may relate to contaminated optics, the laser diode module, the power supply or a protection system limiting operation. Observing treatment results does not replace a technical assessment. [Read more →](https://lasertechservice.pl/en/blog/why-laser-diode-modules-fail/)

### The device overheats

Restricted airflow, a dirty heat exchanger, a faulty fan or coolant circulation problems may impair heat dissipation. Operating conditions must also be considered. [Read more →](https://lasertechservice.pl/en/blog/laser-cooling-system-cleaning-overheating/)

### Error messages appear on the display

A message may relate to temperature, flow, communication or other protective functions; the meaning of a code depends on the model. Please provide the exact code and when it appears.

### No laser pulse

Possible causes include the device not being ready, an active safety interlock, a trigger signal problem, a handpiece connection or the power supply. This does not automatically mean the diode needs replacing.
