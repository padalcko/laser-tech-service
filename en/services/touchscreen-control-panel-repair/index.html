Service scope

Touch panel replacement

We diagnose the touch glass and digitiser. We replace the touch layer separately where the panel design allows it.

Display module replacement

We check LCD/TFT screens, damaged display panels and backlighting. We confirm whether a compatible module can be fitted for the model.

Cables, ribbon cables and connectors

We restore electrical contact, replace damaged ribbon cables and repair connections between the display and control board.

Control panel electronics

We check screen power, the touch controller and circuit boards. We repair identified hardware faults within the device's technical limitations.

When to arrange a service

No response to touch

No display image

Flickering display

Incorrect touch button response

Cracked glass

Diagnostic and service stages

  1. Report and identify the device

    Provide the model, location and symptoms so we can determine the initial inspection scope.

  2. Technical inspection

    We diagnose the touch glass and digitiser. We replace the touch layer separately where the panel design allows it.

  3. Agree the solution

    The final cost of work and replacement parts is agreed with the customer after diagnostics. Parts, laser sources, displays and comprehensive calibration are not automatically included in the starting price.

  4. Post-service checks

    Checks of the image, touch response and connection to the control board.
