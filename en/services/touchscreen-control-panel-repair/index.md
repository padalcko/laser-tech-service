## Service scope

### Touch panel replacement

We diagnose the touch glass and digitiser. We replace the touch layer separately where the panel design allows it.

### Display module replacement

We check LCD/TFT screens, damaged display panels and backlighting. We confirm whether a compatible module can be fitted for the model.

### Cables, ribbon cables and connectors

We restore electrical contact, replace damaged ribbon cables and repair connections between the display and control board.

### Control panel electronics

We check screen power, the touch controller and circuit boards. We repair identified hardware faults within the device's technical limitations.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does cracked glass require a complete screen replacement?

It depends on the design. A separate digitiser can only be replaced if the module allows this repair.

### The image is visible but touch does not work — what do you check?

The touch layer, controller, ribbon cables, connectors and power supply. The symptom alone does not identify the faulty part.

### Can you repair a flickering display?

We diagnose the power supply, connections and display module to establish the source of flickering before replacing parts.

### Will any LCD display fit the device?

No. Electrical specifications, interface, dimensions and control method must match.

### Do you install new software?

Software or firmware work requires a separate assessment of the specific model. It is not automatically included in screen repair.

### Does EUR 165 include a new display?

The price from EUR 165 is the starting service price. The display, digitiser and additional work are agreed separately after diagnostics.
