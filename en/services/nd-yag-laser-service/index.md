## Service scope

### Laser source diagnostics

We check the laser module and pulse generation. In appropriate designs, we diagnose the pump lamp and active element; not every Nd:YAG has the same construction.

### Optical system servicing

We inspect and clean optics and check mirrors, lenses and tips. Alignment is performed where supported by the design and service procedure.

### Energy checks and calibration

We measure output pulse energy with an appropriate instrument and check stability. Calibration feasibility and scope follow manufacturer documentation.

### Electronics and cooling

We diagnose power supplies, control boards, the cooling circuit and electrical connections. Repair covers the agreed faulty components.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is every Nd:YAG a lamp-pumped Q-switched laser?

No. Nd:YAG identifies the active medium; source design and pulse generation method depend on the model.

### Do you replace pump lamps?

In models with a pump lamp, we assess its condition and whether a compatible replacement can be fitted. We do not assume every device contains one.

### How is pulse energy checked?

With an instrument appropriate to the laser output under test. A screen reading does not replace an energy measurement.

### Do you align optics?

Only where the design and documentation permit this work. We assess optical condition first.

### Does reduced performance always mean a damaged source?

No. Optical losses, power supply faults or cooling faults are also possible; diagnostics establish the cause.

### What does the price from EUR 220 mean?

This is the starting service price. The source, lamp, other parts and comprehensive calibration are not automatically included; the quotation is agreed with the customer.
