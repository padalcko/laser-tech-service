Service scope

Laser source diagnostics

We check the laser module and pulse generation. In appropriate designs, we diagnose the pump lamp and active element; not every Nd:YAG has the same construction.

Optical system servicing

We inspect and clean optics and check mirrors, lenses and tips. Alignment is performed where supported by the design and service procedure.

Energy checks and calibration

We measure output pulse energy with an appropriate instrument and check stability. Calibration feasibility and scope follow manufacturer documentation.

Electronics and cooling

We diagnose power supplies, control boards, the cooling circuit and electrical connections. Repair covers the agreed faulty components.
