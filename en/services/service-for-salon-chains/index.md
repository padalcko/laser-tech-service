## Cooperation without a subscription

Laser Tech Service offers tailored service cooperation agreements for beauty salons, aesthetic clinics and multi-location salon chains.

Under a service cooperation agreement, we can provide individually negotiated rates for diagnostics, repairs, component restoration and preventive maintenance of professional aesthetic equipment.

**No subscription fees or fixed monthly payments. You only pay for services actually performed.**

Commercial terms are negotiated individually based on the number and types of devices, facility locations and expected service requirements.

No mandatory packages, minimum work volumes or automatic charges. Visits and their costs are agreed separately unless the agreement provides otherwise.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to pay a monthly subscription fee?

No. There are no subscription fees or fixed monthly payments. You only pay for work actually performed.

### How are special rates determined?

Individually, based on the number and types of devices, facility locations and expected service requirements. There is no minimum work volume.

### Can you service equipment from different manufacturers?

Yes. We can service different types and brands of professional aesthetic equipment. The scope is agreed for the specific models and their technical requirements.

### Do you work with chains in different Polish cities?

Yes. Service conditions for each location are agreed individually. Visits and their costs are agreed separately unless the agreement provides otherwise.

### Is a signed agreement required?

Yes. A cooperation agreement is required to receive individually negotiated corporate terms. It sets the pricing and organisational conditions without introducing a subscription fee.

### Can a single salon apply?

Yes. A single salon can also propose cooperation. Terms are discussed individually.
