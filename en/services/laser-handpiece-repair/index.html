When should a handpiece be checked?

Symptoms are not a final diagnosis. A similar problem can have different causes, so the handpiece and its interaction with the device need technical inspection before parts are selected.

The handpiece has stopped cooling

The cause may involve coolant circulation, heat dissipation, power or controls. We check the Peltier module only in designs that use one.

Laser performance has decreased

Reduced performance may involve the optics, laser diode module or power supply. Treatment results alone are insufficient to assess laser output.

The handpiece overheats

We check the cooling system and thermal connections. We do not assume that replacing thermal paste will be sufficient.

Coolant is leaking

Inspection identifies the leak location and the condition of hoses, connections and seals. Report a leak before using the equipment further.

Damaged seals

Worn or incorrectly seated O-rings and seals may cause leaks. We select parts compatible with the design.

Unstable diode operation

Irregular operation requires checks of the module, its power supply and connections. The symptom does not automatically mean the diode needs replacing.

Contaminated cooling channels

Deposits and contamination may restrict flow. Cleaning scope depends on channel design and access.

What does refurbishment include?

Not every handpiece contains all the components described. We determine the specific repair scope after diagnostics and agree it with the customer.

Laser diode replacement

We diagnose the faulty laser diode module and select a compatible replacement. Replacement feasibility and component cost depend on the model.

Cooling element replacement

We diagnose and replace faulty thermoelectric cooling modules (Peltier/TEC) where used in the handpiece.

Thermal paste replacement

We clean contact surfaces and restore thermal contact between cooling system components according to their design.

Seal and component replacement

We replace damaged O-rings, seals, connections and other faulty parts identified during diagnostics.

Cleaning and sealing

We remove contamination and clean internal components. Suitable silicone sealants are used only where permitted by the manufacturer's design.

How does the repair proceed?

  1. Report a fault

    Provide the model, symptoms and circumstances in which the problem appeared. We will agree equipment handover or a visit.

  2. Handpiece diagnostics

    We check the handpiece and, where needed, its interaction with the device.

  3. Identifying faulty components

    We establish the cause and availability of suitable parts.

  4. Agreeing the quotation

    We agree costs and scope before starting the repair.

  5. Repair or refurbishment

    We carry out the agreed work and replace the identified components.

  6. Post-repair checks

    We check for leaks, cooling and operation within the scope appropriate to the design.

  7. Equipment handover

    We explain the work completed and return the equipment as agreed.
