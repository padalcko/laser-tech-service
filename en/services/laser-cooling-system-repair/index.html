Service scope

Circulation pump diagnostics and repair

We check the pump, coolant circuit and system performance. We identify the cause of low flow and replace faulty components appropriate to the model.

Repairing cooling circuit leaks

We inspect hoses, fittings, connections and seals. We repair leaks and check the circuit for watertightness after reassembly.

Cooling system cleaning

We clean filters, channels, reservoirs and heat exchangers. Circuit flushing is carried out only using a procedure permitted for the specific device.

Cooling sensors and components

We diagnose temperature and flow sensors, fans, heat exchangers and control electronics. The repair scope follows from the fault identified.
