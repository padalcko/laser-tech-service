## Service scope

### Circulation pump diagnostics and repair

We check the pump, coolant circuit and system performance. We identify the cause of low flow and replace faulty components appropriate to the model.

### Repairing cooling circuit leaks

We inspect hoses, fittings, connections and seals. We repair leaks and check the circuit for watertightness after reassembly.

### Cooling system cleaning

We clean filters, channels, reservoirs and heat exchangers. Circuit flushing is carried out only using a procedure permitted for the specific device.

### Cooling sensors and components

We diagnose temperature and flow sensors, fans, heat exchangers and control electronics. The repair scope follows from the fault identified.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a flow error mean the pump is faulty?

Not always. The cause may be a filter, hose, sensor or pump. We check the entire circuit associated with the symptom.

### What coolant can I use in my laser?

Coolant selection and replacement procedures must follow the manufacturer's documentation. We do not recommend a universal fluid for all lasers.

### Can you replace just a leaking hose?

If a compatible part is available and the design allows it, the repair can include replacement and a leak check.

### Is flushing suitable for every design?

No. The type of contamination, circuit materials and model documentation determine the permissible cleaning method.

### Do you check temperature after repair?

We check cooling operation and coolant circulation under test conditions appropriate to the device.
