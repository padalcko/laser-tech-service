## Service scope

### Cooling system repair

We check cooling circuits, pumps, heat exchangers or compressors, depending on the system used. We replace faulty components and check for leaks and cooling performance. Work on the refrigeration circuit is assessed individually.

### Vacuum system repair

In vacuum models, we diagnose pumps, valves and pressure sensors. We check hoses, seals and applicator airtightness. We replace damaged hoses and seals and restore applicator sealing.

### Cryotherapy handpiece and applicator refurbishment

We check cooling plates, temperature sensors and electrical and fluid connections. We replace damaged thermoelectric elements and seals where present in the design. Cryolipolysis handpiece repair may include restoring connections and replacing temperature sensors.

### Electronics repair and temperature checks

Diagnostics of boards, sensors and electronic components. Checks of temperature modes and protective responses after repair. We repair faulty control electronics components.

## When diagnostics are needed

We establish the cause by inspecting the specific device.

### No cooling or uneven cooling

The applicator does not reach the required temperature or cools unevenly.

### No vacuum

The applicator in a vacuum model does not retain suction.

### Temperature errors

The device reports a sensor fault, overheats or shuts down.

### Coolant leakage

A visible leak requires identification of its location and cause.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do cryotherapy and cryolipolysis use the same systems?

No. Designs may differ. We tailor inspection to the specific device and its applicators.

### Does every device have a vacuum pump?

No. Vacuum diagnostics apply only to models equipped with a vacuum system.

### Do you replace Peltier elements?

If an applicator uses thermoelectric elements, we diagnose them and establish whether compatible replacements can be fitted.

### Do you repair every refrigeration circuit?

We assess feasibility after identifying the design and parts availability. We do not claim to repair every circuit type.

### What can cause a temperature error?

The cause may be a sensor, connection, controls or cooling fault. The message alone does not establish a definite cause.

### How much does servicing cost?

Prices start at EUR 330. The precise scope, parts and additional work are quoted after diagnostics.

### Are diagnostics and travel free?

Diagnostics are free when you commission repair or maintenance. Travel is charged separately.

### How do I report a fault?

Provide the model, location, symptoms and error messages. Specify which applicator is affected.
