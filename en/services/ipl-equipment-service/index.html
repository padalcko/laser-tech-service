When does IPL equipment need servicing?

No pulses or reduced treatment results do not automatically identify one faulty component. We establish the cause through diagnostics, considering settings, design and device condition.

No IPL pulses

The device does not flash when triggered. Readiness, controls, the lamp and its power supply need checking.

Reduced treatment effectiveness

A worn lamp may reduce pulse energy. Treatment results alone do not replace a technical inspection of the lamp and optics.

Handpiece overheating

Excessive handpiece heat may result from insufficient heat removal or other faults. Do not continue using an overheating handpiece.

Faulty cooling

Inadequate cooling requires checks of the components used in the specific model, including the coolant circuit where fitted.

Errors and unstable operation

We assess messages, interruptions and irregular pulses against the device documentation. Please provide the error code when contacting us.

Damaged filters or optical components

Cracks, degradation or contamination in the optical path require assessment. Not every damaged component can be cleaned or reused.

What does IPL servicing include?

IPL xenon flashlamp replacement

We diagnose the flashlamp and check handpiece operation. We replace a worn or faulty lamp with a compatible part and then check operation. Lamp wear may reduce light pulse energy.

Cleaning and replacement of optical filters and light guide components

We assess filters and light guide components, clean accessible surfaces using suitable methods and replace damaged or degraded parts. We check the optical path after servicing. Not every filter or light guide can be restored for use.

IPL cooling system diagnostics and repair

We check handpiece cooling and, depending on the design, coolant circulation, pumps, fans and sensors. We address identified faults and check the liquid circuit for leaks where fitted. Scope depends on the cooling design.

IPL pulse energy checks and calibration

We check pulse stability and operating parameters. Output energy is measured where suitable measurement equipment is available. Calibration is possible according to the manufacturer's documentation where the model supports it and a procedure is available. We do not claim to calibrate every IPL device.

From request to IPL testing

  1. Describe device operation

    Provide the model, messages and symptoms: no flash, reduced performance, heating or a cooling problem.

  2. Handpiece and device diagnostics

    We assess the lamp, optics and associated systems within the scope of the reported problem.

  3. Agreeing repair and price

    We confirm feasible work, required parts and available checking or calibration procedures. We agree the quotation with the customer.

  4. Component servicing or replacement

    We carry out approved work on the lamp, optics or cooling as indicated by diagnostics.

  5. Post-service operational test

    We check handpiece operation, cooling and the parameters that can be verified for the model.
