## Service scope

### Cleaning the device interior

We remove dust and accumulated contamination from accessible components, fans and heat sinks. The cleaning method takes sensitive components into account.

### Cooling system maintenance

We check circulation, clean filters, check for leaks and assess the coolant. Fluid selection and maintenance procedures depend on the device documentation.

### Electronics and mechanical components

We inspect contacts, cables, connectors, fans, pumps and drives. Any damage found is discussed with the customer before additional repairs.

### Operating parameter checks

We check functions, safety systems and operating modes appropriate to the model. After maintenance, we check operation; the scope of measurements is determined for the device.

## Frequently asked questions

### How often should maintenance be carried out?

The interval depends on the manufacturer's recommendations, usage intensity and conditions in the clinic. We do not apply one interval to all devices.

### Will maintenance prevent every breakdown?

No. It helps detect contamination and wear early but does not guarantee that future faults will not occur.

### Does cleaning include the inside of the device?

Yes, within the agreed scope of accessible components. We choose the method according to the design and sensitivity of the components.

### Does the coolant always need replacing?

We assess its condition and the requirements in the model's documentation. We do not use a universal fluid or procedure.

### What if you find damage during maintenance?

We inform you, assess the repair needed and agree additional work and parts before proceeding.

### Does the price from EUR 330 include all parts?

This is the starting maintenance price. Materials, parts and additional repairs depend on the device's condition and the agreed quotation.
