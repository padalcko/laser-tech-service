Aesthetic equipment cleaning and maintenance

Scheduled maintenance helps identify contamination, overheating and component wear at an early stage. We adapt cleaning and inspection to the device design and its operating conditions.

Service scope

Cleaning the device interior

We remove dust and accumulated contamination from accessible components, fans and heat sinks. The cleaning method takes sensitive components into account.

Cooling system maintenance

We check circulation, clean filters, check for leaks and assess the coolant. Fluid selection and maintenance procedures depend on the device documentation.

Electronics and mechanical components

We inspect contacts, cables, connectors, fans, pumps and drives. Any damage found is discussed with the customer before additional repairs.

Operating parameter checks

We check functions, safety systems and operating modes appropriate to the model. After maintenance, we check operation; the scope of measurements is determined for the device.
