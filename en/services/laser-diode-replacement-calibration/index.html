Service scope

Laser diode module diagnostics

We check reduced power, module condition and electrical parameters. We establish whether the diode is responsible and whether replacement is necessary.

Laser diode module and stack replacement

We remove the damaged module, select a compatible diode or stack and check mechanical and electrical connections. Availability of a suitable component is confirmed for the model. We install the new compatible component.

Restoring laser diode module cooling

We check heat dissipation, the thermal interface where present and the coolant circuit for leaks. Operating temperature is checked after repair. Where needed, we renew the thermal interface provided for by the design.

Calibration and output power measurement

We measure actual optical power with an appropriate instrument, compare it with settings and perform adjustments supported by the device. A screen value is not an optical power measurement; restored parameters must be confirmed by measurement. We carry out a verification test after replacement.

When diagnostics are needed

We establish the cause by inspecting the specific device.

Reduced results or output

Reduced hair removal results, no emission or low optical power require diagnostics.

Unstable operation

The module operates irregularly or the device reports errors.

Overheating

Heat dissipation problems may affect diode operation.

Failure following a leak

After overheating or leakage, the module and the cause of damage need examination.

How the repair works
