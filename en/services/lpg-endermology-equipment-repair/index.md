## Service scope

### Rollers and drives

We check roller mechanisms and drive operation. We repair drives and replace worn rollers, shafts and compatible mechanical components.

### Vacuum system

We check vacuum pumps, hoses, valves and filters. We locate leaks and restore the system's seal within the agreed scope.

### Handpiece refurbishment

We repair cables, electrical connectors, contacts and control buttons. We also check the handpiece's mechanical components and its connection to the device.

### Seals and consumable parts

We replace damaged seals, rings, bushings and other worn components appropriate to the model. After reassembly, we check the repaired assemblies.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does weak suction always mean a faulty pump?

No. Hoses, filters, valves or seals may be responsible. We check the circuit before deciding to replace the pump.

### Why do the rollers stop turning?

There may be a drive fault, mechanism wear or a control problem. We establish the cause when inspecting the handpiece.

### Can you repair a handpiece the device does not recognise?

We check the cable, connectors, contacts and electronics. Repair feasibility depends on the damage found and parts availability.

### Do parts fit every LPG device?

No. Selection requires identification of the model and handpiece design; availability is confirmed individually.

### Is this an authorised LPG Systems service?

We do not claim authorisation from LPG Systems. We describe the technical services provided by Laser Tech Service.

### How do you price roller or vacuum system repair?

Servicing starts at EUR 220. The final work and parts are agreed after diagnostics of the mechanism and vacuum system.
