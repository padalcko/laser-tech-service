## Service scope

### Source diagnostics

We check pulse generation, laser module operation and controls. Source repairability is assessed according to its design and service access.

### Optical path servicing

We inspect optical components and energy losses. We clean only surfaces permitted for servicing; alignment depends on model capabilities.

### Pulse energy and calibration

We measure energy with a compatible instrument and check stability. Calibration is performed within the documented scope; energy measurement is not pulse duration measurement.

### Electronics and cooling repair

We diagnose power, control boards, cooling, sensors and connections. We repair agreed components after assessing the fault's cause.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you repair every picosecond source?

No. Sealed or non-repairable modules may require a different solution. We assess repair feasibility before offering it.

### Do you measure picosecond pulse duration?

We do not claim this measurement without confirmed specialist equipment. Measuring pulse energy does not confirm pulse duration.

### Can all optical components be cleaned?

No. We clean only accessible surfaces whose servicing is permitted by the model documentation.

### Could reduced energy be related to cooling?

This may be one diagnostic area. We also check the source, optical path and controls.

### Is calibration always possible?

It depends on the model, documentation and available procedures and instruments. Scope is confirmed before work.

### Does the price from EUR 330 include the laser source?

It is not the price of a new source or comprehensive calibration. Parts and agreed work are quoted after diagnostics.
