## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a laser repair take?

Most repairs are completed within 1–3 working days. More complex faults may take longer; we will let you know in advance.

### Do you provide a warranty on repairs?

Yes. Every repair is covered by a warranty. Its duration depends on the equipment and the work carried out, typically 3–6 months.

### How do I request equipment servicing?

You can call us, contact us on WhatsApp or use the service request form. Describe the problem and, where possible, send photographs of the equipment.

### Can I send equipment by courier?

Yes. The equipment must be properly protected for transport and include your contact details. After the repair, we will return it to the address you provide.

### Do you offer free diagnostics?

Diagnostics are free if you proceed with the repair. Otherwise, an assessment fee may apply.

### Do you use original parts?

We use original parts or proven, high-quality alternatives, depending on availability, the equipment and the client's preference.
