## Why is regular servicing necessary?

Laser equipment generates heat during operation. Removing that heat matters for maintaining the intended operating conditions. Maintenance should therefore not depend solely on an error message appearing.

Inspections assess device condition against its documentation. Scope and intervals should be tailored to the model, usage intensity and clinic conditions.

## The importance of cooling

In a liquid-cooled system, coolant absorbs heat and the circuit carries it to components that release it into the surroundings. Suitable flow, temperature control and leak-tightness matter as much as fluid level. A full reservoir does not itself establish correct circulation.

Fluid type and replacement requirements should follow the specific device's documentation. This description explains cooling's role; it is not a measurement report.
