LONGER Laser Engraver Calibration for Cutting and Engraving

2026-11-25

Laser Tech Service received a LONGER laser engraver used for cutting plywood and engraving materials. The customer contacted our service team to have the equipment calibrated and its proper operation restored.

Although aesthetic laser equipment represents a significant part of our business, our technical expertise also extends to laser systems used for material processing.

Laser engraver diagnostics

The equipment underwent a technical inspection. The correct operation of a laser engraver depends not only on its laser module but also on its mechanical components, control system and software configuration.

Incorrect settings can affect processing accuracy, repeatability, cutting performance and engraving quality.

Diagnostics must therefore consider both hardware and software.

Technical and software calibration

Following the inspection, Laser Tech Service specialists resolved the reported problem and calibrated the equipment.

The service included:

  • assessment of the laser engraver's technical condition;
  • diagnostics of the reported issue;
  • troubleshooting;
  • technical calibration;
  • software configuration and calibration;
  • post-service operational testing.

Following the service, the equipment was tested while processing material.

Service result

The reported issue was resolved, and the equipment was calibrated at both the technical and software levels.

The laser engraver was restored to operation.

Technical and industrial laser servicing

Laser Tech Service works with more than aesthetic laser equipment. We also accept service enquiries concerning laser engravers, CNC laser machines and other laser systems used in commercial and manufacturing applications.

The scope of service is determined individually according to the equipment design and reported issue.
