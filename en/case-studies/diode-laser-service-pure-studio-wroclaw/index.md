Laser Tech Service recently completed another on-site service visit for our long-standing client, PURE STUDIO in Wrocław. The work included replacing the laser handpiece housing and control button, cleaning internal components and optical surfaces, resealing the handpiece, servicing the diode module and verifying laser output parameters.

## On-Site Service at a Laser Hair Removal Studio

PURE STUDIO is a Wrocław-based beauty studio offering laser hair removal treatments.

We have worked with the studio for an extended period and appreciate the attention its owners give to equipment maintenance and technical reliability.

During this visit, our technicians serviced a diode laser handpiece that required replacement housing components and a new control button.

The service also included preventive maintenance of internal assemblies and optical components.

![Disassembled handpiece housing and internal components](https://lasertechservice.pl/img/realizacje/pure-studio/wymiana-obudowy-manipuly.webp)

## Handpiece Housing and Control Button Replacement

We began by carefully disassembling the handpiece to access its internal components.

The housing and control button were replaced, with particular attention paid to component positioning, cable routing and mechanical connections.

The handpiece housing protects sensitive internal parts against physical damage and contamination. A properly functioning control button is essential for reliable operation during treatments.

![Laser handpiece disassembly and access to internal components](https://lasertechservice.pl/img/realizacje/pure-studio/naprawa-manipuly-lasera-diodowego.webp)

## Internal Cleaning and Laser Optics Maintenance

Once the handpiece was disassembled, we cleaned its internal components and accessible optical surfaces, including the laser output glass.

Contamination on optical surfaces can cause additional energy losses, localized heating and less favorable operating conditions for the laser assembly.

For this reason, optical cleaning is an important part of professional diode laser maintenance.

The cleaning process was carried out with consideration for the sensitivity of optical surfaces.

## Diode Module Maintenance and Handpiece Sealing

The next stage involved servicing the diode laser assembly, reassembling the handpiece and restoring its sealing.

We checked component positioning and mechanical connections and ensured that the handpiece was properly sealed.

Effective sealing helps reduce the risk of moisture and contamination entering sensitive internal areas.

This is particularly important for equipment used regularly in professional laser hair removal studios.

![Laser handpiece measurement using a LaserPoint instrument](https://lasertechservice.pl/img/realizacje/pure-studio/pomiar-mocy-lasera-laserpoint-wroclaw.webp)

## Laser Output Power Verification

After completing the maintenance work, we performed a measurement of laser emission parameters using a specialized LaserPoint measuring instrument.

Measuring actual laser output provides valuable information about diode module performance beyond the values displayed on the equipment screen.

Our assessment considered the laser operating settings, measurement conditions and applicable principles for evaluating laser equipment.

The results confirmed that the diode module was operating correctly under the tested conditions and that the measured parameters met the service acceptance criteria applied during the inspection.

A service measurement does not replace full equipment certification or comprehensive regulatory conformity testing.

## Service Results

The completed work included handpiece housing and button replacement, internal cleaning, optical maintenance, diode module servicing, handpiece resealing and laser output verification.

Following reassembly, the service inspection confirmed correct handpiece operation within the tested conditions.
