## Scheduled medical laser maintenance

The service concerned a **Pharaon 1470 from LSO Medical** operating at the medical clinic **Denica in Wrocław**. This unit's rating plate states the manufacture date as **2014-08**. This provides a reference for identifying the device and maintaining its service history.

The purpose was to assess technical condition and perform scheduled maintenance. It was not a request to replace the laser module or repair a specific reported fault. The scope matched the needs of a routine medical laser inspection.

![Technician unscrewing the Pharaon 1470 side cover](https://lasertechservice.pl/img/realizacje/pharaon-1470/pharaon-1470-demontaz-oslony.webp)

## Diagnostics and internal access

We began with comprehensive diagnostics and a technical condition check. We opened the housing to gain the internal access needed for inspection and maintenance.

A well-kept housing does not reveal everything beneath the covers. Dust can accumulate inside over time, unseen during daily use. Condition assessment therefore goes beyond examining the screen and external surfaces.

![Work on the Pharaon 1470 housing during maintenance](https://lasertechservice.pl/img/realizacje/pharaon-1470/pharaon-1470-prace-przy-obudowie.webp)

## Cleaning and scheduled maintenance

We removed accumulated dust and contamination from inside the device and completed its scheduled maintenance. Cleaning was part of the maintenance alongside diagnostics and condition checks.

Regular medical equipment maintenance helps monitor condition even when the equipment still works. It is not a promise of fault-free operation, but an organised way to care for equipment and identify changes needing attention.

![Technician with documentation beside the Pharaon 1470](https://lasertechservice.pl/img/realizacje/pharaon-1470/pharaon-1470-dokumentacja-serwisowa.webp)

## Post-service checks and documentation

After completing the work, we checked the device. The maintenance was recorded in its service log/documentation.

This record organises the work history: at the next service, earlier tasks can be reviewed rather than relying solely on the user's memory. Documentation is part of equipment servicing, not an optional addition.

## Pharaon 1470 ready for continued use

After diagnostics, cleaning, full scheduled maintenance and final checks, the device was ready for continued use at Denica in Wrocław. The visit's scope was recorded in the service documentation.

This case shows that medical laser servicing in Wrocław includes regular maintenance, not only interventions after a breakdown.
