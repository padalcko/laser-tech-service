SERVICE REQUEST

Diagnostics and technical preparation

A customer in Legnica commissioned diagnostics and routine technical preparation of a tattoo removal laser .

The device underwent technical inspection and maintenance to assess its condition and prepare it for continued use.

Servicing was carried out at the customer's premises in Legnica.

WORK SCOPE

What was done?

During diagnostics and maintenance, we performed the following tasks:

  • technical device inspection
  • general component condition checks
  • cooling system inspection
  • cleaning the interior and components requiring maintenance
  • removing accumulated contamination
  • checking filter condition
  • removing the used filter
  • installing a new filter
  • checking ventilation and cooling components
  • final device inspection after the work
COOLING SYSTEM

Cooling system inspection and maintenance

Cooling is an important part of professional laser equipment.

Contamination can accumulate inside during use, gradually worsening operating conditions for components.

During this job, we paid particular attention to components responsible for cooling, ventilation and filtration.

The used filter was replaced and system components cleaned before restarting the device.

PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE

Why does filter replacement matter?

Regular inspection of consumable components helps maintain the intended operating conditions.

01

Improved flow

A clean filter helps maintain correct flow in a system requiring filtration.

02

More stable cooling

A cleaned system operates under more favourable thermal conditions.

03

Reduced load

Regular maintenance reduces unnecessary loading of cooling components.

04

Preventing breakdowns

Routine servicing can identify potential problems before a more serious failure occurs.

SERVICE RESULT

Device prepared for continued use

After diagnostics, cleaning, maintenance and filter replacement, the device was inspected again.

The work prepared the laser for continued operation and reduced the risk of problems caused by contamination of consumable components and the cooling system.
