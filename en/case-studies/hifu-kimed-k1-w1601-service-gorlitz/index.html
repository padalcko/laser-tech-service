SERVICE REQUEST

Servicing a HIFU KIMED K1-W1601

Laser Tech Service carried out maintenance on a KIMED K1-W1601 at the customer's premises in Görlitz, Germany.

This is a professional High Intensity Focused Ultrasonic Unit — HIFU. One reported problem was a non-working handpiece requiring diagnostics and repair.

During maintenance, we also checked the device interior and water system. The reservoir contained accumulated contamination and deposits, so it was thoroughly cleaned.

WORK SCOPE

What was done?

The work included resolving the handpiece fault and carrying out routine device maintenance.

  • technical diagnostics of the HIFU device
  • inspection of the non-working HIFU handpiece
  • handpiece repair
  • checking handpiece operation after repair
  • inspection of the device interior
  • water system inspection
  • cleaning the water system reservoir
  • removal of accumulated contamination and deposits
  • inspection of accessible water circuit components
  • device reassembly
  • final device operational test
HIFU HANDPIECE REPAIR

Non-working handpiece

One of the main reasons for the service visit was a non-working HIFU handpiece.

We diagnosed the problem and repaired the handpiece. After completing the work, we checked its operation with the device.

The repaired handpiece was checked before the final test of the complete KIMED K1-W1601 system.

WATER SYSTEM

Reservoir cleaning and water circuit inspection

During maintenance, contamination and deposits were visible in the water system reservoir.

The reservoir was thoroughly cleaned and accessible water circuit components inspected before restarting the device.

Regular inspection and cleaning of these systems is an important part of maintaining equipment that uses a water circuit during operation.

Repair and maintenance during one visit

During the visit, we resolved the reported fault and carried out routine equipment maintenance.
