Laser cooling fan replacement and device maintenance — Wrocław

Aesthetic laser device · Wrocław

Inadequate cooling of an aesthetic laser required an on-site visit in Wrocław. Diagnostics identified faulty fans and substantial contamination inside the device. We replaced the fans, cleaned the internal components and checked cooling operation after repair.

Reported fault: the cooling system was ineffective

A client in Wrocław reported inadequate cooling during device operation. Laser Tech Service arranged a prompt specialist visit to the device’s location. The aim was to establish the cause and restore system operation, rather than replace parts solely on the basis of a symptom.

This case concerns an aesthetic laser device. The available photographs do not conclusively establish its manufacturer or model, so we do not assign a trade name, technology, power rating or wavelength to it.

Diagnostics: faulty fans and dust inside the housing

Cooling system diagnostics identified damaged fans. There was a substantial amount of dust and other contamination inside the device. The photograph shows a dusty grille beside a fan and access to this assembly during servicing.

In this case, accumulated contamination impaired ventilation and contributed to fan failure. These findings concern this specific job; they do not mean that dust is the only possible cause of every cooling fault.

In general, deposits restrict the airflow path and can impair heat dissipation. Simply removing a faulty fan without cleaning the system would leave part of the problem unresolved. We explain these mechanisms separately in the maintenance article.

Removal and selection of suitable fans

After identifying the fault, we removed the faulty fans and selected suitable replacement components. We then installed them in the device’s cooling system. The photograph shows the fans prepared for the work beside the device handpiece.

A suitable replacement must match the device’s design — a similar housing is not enough. General selection criteria include power supply, mounting and connection compatibility, and airflow characteristics. We do not publish unconfirmed component specifications or attribute airflow or pressure measurements to this repair.

Installation and electrical connections

We installed the selected fans and completed work on their connections. The photograph shows the fan wiring being soldered. It documents a specific servicing task and is not a guide to replacing fans yourself.

Connections must follow the documentation for the particular device. Wire count or colour does not provide universal pinout information. Access to internal components and electrical work require a qualified specialist following the relevant safety procedures.

Internal cleaning and maintenance

In addition to replacing the fans, we removed dust and contamination from the internal components and maintained the device. The work therefore addressed both the faulty parts and the contamination impairing cooling conditions.

The documentation does not establish the specific cleaning products, any airflow measurement method or additional components replaced. We do not add these activities to the description of the work carried out.

Checks after repair

After completing replacement and cleaning, we checked cooling system operation. The system’s function was restored and the device operates correctly.

This is the confirmed service outcome, not a claim of a specific temperature reduction or percentage increase in airflow. The job documentation contains no such measurement results.

Cooling function restored

The laser service visit in Wrocław covered diagnostics, removal of the faulty fans, replacement selection and installation, internal cleaning, maintenance and checks after repair. Both the fan failure and accumulated contamination were addressed. The device returned to correct operation.
