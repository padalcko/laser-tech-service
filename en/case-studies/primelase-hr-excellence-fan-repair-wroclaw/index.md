## Reported symptoms

A Primelase HR Excellence was received by Laser Tech Service following the appearance of abnormal mechanical noise, rattling and reduced heat dissipation from inside the equipment housing.

The reported symptoms required inspection of the cooling fan assembly and heat exchange system.

## Fault diagnosis

After accessing the internal components, a failed bearing was identified in the main radiator cooling fan.

This fan provides forced airflow through the heat exchanger serving the thermoelectric heat rejection circuit.

Bearing deterioration was identified as the source of the reported mechanical noise. Additional inspection revealed dust and operational debris on the radiator surface.

![Inside the device and cooling system](https://lasertechservice.pl/img/realizacje/primelase-hr-excellence/primelase-internal-cooling-system.webp)

## Cooling fan bearing replacement

![Work on the fan](https://lasertechservice.pl/img/realizacje/primelase-hr-excellence/primelase-fan-bearing-repair.webp)

The fan assembly was removed and the failed bearing was replaced.

A component-level repair was performed instead of replacing the complete fan and radiator assembly.

The purpose of the intervention was to restore normal rotational mechanics and address resistance and vibration associated with the defective bearing.

![Radiator cooling fan](https://lasertechservice.pl/img/realizacje/primelase-hr-excellence/primelase-radiator-cooling-fan.webp)

## Radiator cleaning and equipment maintenance

![Fan and contaminated radiator](https://lasertechservice.pl/img/realizacje/primelase-hr-excellence/primelase-radiator-dust-contamination.webp)

Comprehensive equipment maintenance was carried out alongside the mechanical repair, including removal of dust and contamination from the internal housing and heat exchange surfaces.

Radiator cleanliness is particularly important in equipment using thermoelectric cooling for laser diodes and applicator contact tips.

Restricted airflow may impair overall heat rejection even when the hydraulic circuit components remain operational.

![Fan maintenance](https://lasertechservice.pl/img/realizacje/primelase-hr-excellence/primelase-fan-maintenance.webp)

## Completed work

- Assessment of the reported symptoms.

- Identification of the failed bearing.

- Removal of the cooling fan assembly.

- Replacement of the damaged bearing.

- Radiator cleaning.

- Removal of internal dust and contamination.

- Comprehensive technical maintenance.

## Repair outcome

The identified mechanical fault was addressed through bearing replacement, accompanied by maintenance of the heat rejection system.

Replacement of the entire fan and radiator assembly was not required.
