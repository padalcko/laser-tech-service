## Reported fault: fluid leaked when connecting the handpiece

The owner of a Lumenis LightSheer DESIRE reported coolant leaking from the connector when attaching the handpiece. The device also had temperature problems: cooling was inadequate, the device overheated and could not operate normally. Laser Tech Service arranged a technical visit to the customer in Oleśnica, Lower Silesia.

The work concerned a professional diode laser for hair reduction, used for approximately ten years. This approximate age does not establish its exact year of manufacture. Around PLN 200,000 is an indicative value for equipment in this class, not a current valuation of the repaired unit.

## Diagnosing the circuit and hydraulic connector

After removing the protective panels, the specialist gained access to internal components. He checked the cooling circuit and handpiece connection assembly. Heavy contamination was found, including in the filter element and non-return valve. The mesh had clearly visible hard deposits resembling mineral scale.

The photograph shows accumulated contamination but does not establish its chemical composition. Without analysis, neither the compounds forming the deposits nor the previously used fluid can be proven. Identification of the problem was based on device diagnostics, not solely the photograph's appearance.

## Non-return valve and O-rings

Contamination can restrict flow and impede the non-return valve, which controls flow direction. In this job, contamination of the assembly was a significant factor in the malfunction. The O-rings were also checked: the hydraulic connector seals were not providing the required seal.

The problem therefore involved concurrent contamination, incorrect valve operation and leaking elastomer seals. We do not attribute unconfirmed chemical damage to the rubber to these deposits. Restoring operation required attention to the complete inspected connection, not simply topping up the lost liquid.

Hard deposits cover part of the mesh surface. Such contamination may restrict flow in the cooling circuit. The precise composition of the deposits was not determined by analysis.
