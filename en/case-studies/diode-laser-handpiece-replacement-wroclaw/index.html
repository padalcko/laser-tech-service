SERVICE REQUEST

Diode laser maintenance

In Wrocław, we maintained a professional diode laser used for hair removal treatments.

The main task was treatment handpiece replacement. We also performed routine maintenance and cleaned the device.

The device was opened during the work, providing access to its interior and allowing accessible components to be inspected.

WORK SCOPE

What was done?

We carried out comprehensive servicing as part of one job.

  • technical diode laser inspection
  • preparing the device for service work
  • removing the existing treatment handpiece
  • replacing the diode laser handpiece
  • checking connections
  • inspecting accessible device components
  • cleaning the device
  • removing accumulated contamination
  • device reassembly
  • checking handpiece operation
  • final device operational checks
TREATMENT HANDPIECE

Diode laser handpiece replacement

Replacing the treatment handpiece was one of the most important stages of the work.

After fitting, we checked connections and correct installation of the new handpiece.

After reassembling the device, we checked its operation.

CLEANING

Device cleaning

We also cleaned the device as part of maintenance.

Accumulated contamination was removed from accessible components requiring routine maintenance.

After cleaning, the device was prepared for reassembly and operational checks.
