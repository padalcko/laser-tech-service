SERVICE REQUEST

A diode laser handpiece with a failed seal

During a service visit to a customer in Poznań, we inspected the handpiece of a professional diode hair removal laser.

The problem concerned the handpiece housing. Damage had compromised its seal, allowing dust and other fine contaminants inside.

In equipment operating with high optical energy, even minor contamination inside the handpiece should not be ignored.

DIAGNOSTICS

What did we find?

After thorough inspection and dismantling, we confirmed that the handpiece interior needed cleaning.

  • loss of the housing's seal
  • contaminants entering the handpiece
  • deposits and dust inside the handpiece
  • contamination around the optical system
  • internal handpiece components requiring inspection
ON-SITE SERVICING

Dismantling the handpiece and accessing the interior

The service was carried out at the customer's premises in Poznań.

The handpiece was dismantled to access its internal components and assess the extent of contamination thoroughly.

This allowed cleaning of areas inaccessible during normal device use.

INSIDE THE HANDPIECE

Contamination inside the handpiece

Opening the handpiece revealed contamination that had entered through the failed seal.

In a correctly assembled handpiece, the housing helps limit dust and other contaminants reaching sensitive internal components.

After any housing damage or loss of sealing, the condition inside the handpiece should therefore be checked.

OPTICS

Optical system inspection and cleaning

Components around the optical path required particular attention.

Contamination on optical components can affect energy transmission and cause additional heating during laser operation.

Before reassembling the handpiece, we therefore thoroughly removed contamination and inspected accessible components.

LASER DIODE MODULE

Inspecting the laser diode module area

During servicing, we also accessed the laser diode module area and components inside the handpiece.

When sealing has failed, removing visible dust from the housing is not enough: areas deeper inside the handpiece also need inspection.

WORK SCOPE

What was done?

The service included dismantling the handpiece, inspecting its interior, removing contamination and correctly securing the housing again.

01

Dismantling

Dismantling the handpiece to access internal components.

02

Cleaning

Thorough removal of accumulated dust and contamination inside the handpiece.

03

Inspection

Checking the optical area, laser diode module and other accessible handpiece components.

04

Sealing

Correct housing reassembly, restoration of the handpiece seal and securing the joint with suitable sealing material.

SERVICE RESULT

The handpiece was cleaned and resealed

After the work, accumulated contamination had been removed from the handpiece interior.

After correctly reassembling the housing, we restored the handpiece seal and secured the joint with suitable sealing material.

The handpiece was prepared for continued operation.

Why is handpiece sealing so important?

This case shows that a problem does not always begin with damage to the laser diode module itself.

Sometimes the initial cause is a small housing leak through which contaminants gradually enter.
