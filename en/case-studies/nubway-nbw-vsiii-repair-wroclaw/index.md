## Device diagnostics and comprehensive repair

We were called to a **rehabilitation studio in Wrocław**, where a **NUBWAY NBW-VSIII 5D** required diagnostics and several service tasks.

The problems included the vacuum system and the RF component responsible for heating. The condition of the hoses also required service attention.

Given the scope of the request, we carried out a comprehensive device inspection, repaired the identified components and cleaned the internal assemblies.

![Rear of the NUBWAY NBW-VSIII with connections and reservoirs](https://lasertechservice.pl/img/realizacje/naprawa-nubway-nbw-vsiii-wroclaw-1.webp)

## What was done?

After diagnostics, we began servicing several independent device systems.

- vacuum system inspection

- replacement of suction system hoses

- RF component diagnostics

- repair of the RF heating component

- cleaning the device interior

- cleaning components requiring maintenance

- checking device operation after servicing

## Hose replacement and vacuum repair

One of the main areas of work was the system responsible for generating and transmitting vacuum.

The hoses inside the device required replacement. They were removed and replaced with new parts.

The vacuum-generating component was also repaired. After the work, we checked the connections and system operation.

A working vacuum system is essential for correct operation of device functions that use suction during treatments.

## Repairing the RF heating component

Another important part of the work was diagnosing the RF system component responsible for heating.

After locating the problem, we repaired the component responsible for correct heating operation.

After the work, the component was checked again and device operation tested.
