Complete 808 nm Diode Laser Maintenance in Warsaw – Cooling System Service and Handpiece Sealing

Published: 5 November 2026HS-A0406 · 808 nm

Short description

Laser Tech Service carried out scheduled maintenance of an 808 nm diode laser for a long-term customer in Warsaw. The work included coolant replacement, internal cleaning, laser diode power verification and handpiece housing sealing.

Regular maintenance for a long-term customer

Our technicians recently visited one of our regular customers in Warsaw.

This customer follows a disciplined maintenance schedule and arranges comprehensive servicing every six months.

The equipment was an 808 nm diode laser, model HS-A0406.

Although the machine is no longer new, regular preventive maintenance helps keep it in good technical condition.

Cooling system inspection

We began with a technical inspection of the equipment and its main components.

Particular attention was given to the water cooling system, which removes heat generated during laser operation.

The old coolant was drained and replaced with fresh coolant. Accessible cooling circuit components and connections were also inspected.

Regular coolant replacement helps reduce the risk of deposits and contamination that could restrict liquid circulation and reduce cooling efficiency.

Internal cleaning

The next step was to clean dust and dirt from accessible internal components.

Over time, cosmetic laser equipment can accumulate contamination on cooling fans, electronic assemblies and internal surfaces.

Excessive dust may restrict airflow and contribute to higher component temperatures.

Regular cleaning helps reduce these risks.

Laser diode power verification

Our technicians also checked the laser diode power.

Monitoring laser output is an important part of evaluating the technical condition of diode laser equipment.

The energy settings displayed on a machine do not necessarily represent its actual optical output.

Handpiece housing sealing

We also inspected the laser handpiece housing.

With prolonged use, housing joints may lose their sealing effectiveness. Dust, moisture and other contaminants can enter through gaps.

Contamination near sensitive optical components and the diode module may reduce performance or contribute to damage.

The handpiece housing was sealed to reduce the risk of contamination entering the assembly.

Service result

The scheduled maintenance was completed, including coolant replacement, internal cleaning, diode power verification and handpiece sealing.

This service visit demonstrates why preventive maintenance is an important part of long-term laser equipment care.
