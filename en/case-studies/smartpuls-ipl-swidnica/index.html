smartPULS IPL Handpiece Repair in Świdnica

Published: 6 November 2026Estimated reading time: 2 minutes.

Professional IPL equipment diagnostics and repair | Świdnica, Lower Silesia

A client from Świdnica contacted Laser Tech Service after the IPL handpiece of a German-made smartPULS cosmetic device stopped working. The client initially suspected that the IPL lamp had burned out and needed replacement.

Our technicians visited the client's premises to inspect the equipment and identify the cause.

Diagnostics revealed that the lamp was intact

The initial inspection showed no visible signs of a burned-out lamp. Further diagnostics were necessary to understand why the device would no longer operate the handpiece.

The investigation revealed that smartPULS incorporates a maintenance mechanism associated with the number of pulses delivered. The device documentation specifies lamp replacement after 30,000 pulses.

In this case, the interruption was not caused by an obvious physical failure of the lamp but by a mechanism restricting further handpiece operation.

Restoring the equipment

After examining the service-related operating mechanism, Laser Tech Service technicians identified the problem and performed the necessary repair work.

The equipment was restored to operation without replacing the lamp solely on the basis of the initial assumption that it had burned out.

This case demonstrates the importance of professional diagnostics before purchasing expensive replacement components.
