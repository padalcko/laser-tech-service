Overheating Laser Handpiece Repair in Lubin — Diode Wiring Correction

Laser hair removal device

CityLubin
EquipmentLaser hair removal device
Work performedWire shortening and diode reconnection

Problem

Laser Tech Service received a service request from Lubin, Poland, concerning an overheating laser hair removal handpiece.

Diagnosis

During the inspection, our technicians identified excessively long electrical wires connected to the laser diode. In this particular configuration, the wiring contributed to the overheating problem.

Repair

Our technicians shortened the wires and reconnected the laser diode correctly.

After reconnecting the diode, our technicians checked the handpiece operation.

Result

Following the repair, the handpiece stopped overheating and the device returned to normal operation.
