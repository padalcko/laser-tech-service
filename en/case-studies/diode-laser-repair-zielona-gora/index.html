Diode Laser Repair in Zielona Góra. Water Flow Sensor Replacement

A diode hair removal laser at a beauty salon in Zielona Góra stopped operating because of a cooling system monitoring fault. During an on-site inspection, Laser Tech Service technicians identified a defective water flow sensor.

20 November 2026

The problem

When a diode laser stops working, a beauty salon may have to cancel appointments and lose revenue.

Rather than shipping the equipment to a workshop and waiting several days, the customer requested an on-site repair.

Our technicians reached Zielona Góra after approximately two hours of travel.

Diagnosing the cooling system

We opened the equipment housing and inspected the cooling circuit.

The defective component was identified as a MOJO H783C-24V water flow sensor.

This sensor provides the control system with information about coolant circulation.

If the controller does not receive a valid flow signal, it may prevent laser emission to protect the equipment from operating without adequate cooling.

Repair and maintenance

We replaced the faulty water flow sensor and renewed the coolant.

We also performed equipment maintenance, disassembled and cleaned the laser handpiece, and checked the operation of the laser diode.

After completing the repair, we verified the equipment's operation and documented the work in the service record.

Result

The on-site repair took approximately two hours.

The laser was restored to working condition, allowing the salon to resume treatments.

This case demonstrates how a relatively small cooling system component can prevent an entire laser device from operating.
