Beauty Planet 3D Contour repair — vacuum and RF

Beauty Planet 3D Contour

Diagnostics and repair of a multifunctional Beauty Planet 3D Contour aesthetic device. The fault concerned the handpiece: neither the vacuum system nor the RF function worked.

SERVICE REQUEST

Vacuum and RF were not working

We received a device for diagnostics: Beauty Planet 3D Contour, a multifunctional aesthetic system used, among other applications, for body contouring treatments.

The problem concerned one treatment handpiece. During operation, neither the vacuum system nor the RF function worked.

Both the handpiece itself and its connection to the device needed checking.

DIAGNOSTICS

Handpiece power supply problem

During diagnostics, we checked handpiece operation and the functions dependent on its power supply.

We established that the handpiece was not receiving power correctly. As a result, the vacuum and RF functions did not work.

  • incorrect power supply to the handpiece
  • vacuum system not operating
  • RF function not operating
  • handpiece electrical connections requiring inspection
VACUUM AND RF

Checking handpiece functions

After repair, we checked the functions responsible for handpiece operation.

The Beauty Planet 3D Contour allows vacuum levels and RF parameters to be adjusted directly through its interface.

After restoring the correct power supply, we tested the repaired functions.

WORK SCOPE

What was done?

Servicing included handpiece diagnostics and restoration of its functions.

01

Diagnostics

Checking the device, handpiece, vacuum and RF functions.

02

Power supply

Locating the problem preventing correct power delivery to the handpiece.

03

Repair

Restoring correct handpiece operation and its functions.

04

Test

Checking vacuum and RF operation after repair.

EQUIPMENT

Beauty Planet 3D Contour

The serviced unit was a Beauty Planet 3D Contour. According to its rating plate, it operates at 220–240 V, 50/60 Hz and has an input power rating of 1200 VA.

It is a multifunctional aesthetic system with several treatment technologies and a set of interchangeable handpieces.

This repair concerned the handpiece using vacuum and RF.

SERVICE RESULT

Vacuum and RF working correctly again

After identifying the cause, we repaired the handpiece and restored correct operation.

We tested the vacuum system and RF function after completing the work.

The device was checked after servicing and its repaired functions could be used again.
