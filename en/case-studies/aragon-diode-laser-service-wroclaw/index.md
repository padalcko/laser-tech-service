## Maintenance performed

Our technicians inspected the cooling filter, replaced the used filter element, renewed the cooling water and checked the relevant tubing connections.

The device was checked after the maintenance work.

The removed filter showed visible discoloration and signs of contamination. However, visual inspection alone cannot establish the chemical or biological composition of the deposits.

## Why cooling filters matter

Diode lasers generate heat during operation. Their cooling systems help transfer that heat away from sensitive components.

A contaminated filter may restrict coolant flow, potentially reducing cooling efficiency and increasing the risk of overheating.

Over time, inadequate cooling can contribute to premature component wear, operating interruptions and expensive repairs.

## Cooling water quality

The cooling medium must comply with the specifications of the equipment manufacturer.

Although purified water is used in many diode laser systems, the correct type, conductivity and replacement interval depend on the specific device.

Ordinary tap water should not be used unless explicitly permitted by the manufacturer.
