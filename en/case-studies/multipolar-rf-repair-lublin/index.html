SERVICE CASE · LUBLIN

Multipolar RF equipment repair in Lublin

Multipolar RF

On-site aesthetic equipment servicing at a beauty studio in Lublin. The customer reported very weak suction and no heating from the RF element. Diagnostics identified two independent faults: a leak in a damaged pump and a damaged contact in the RF connector.

Multipolar RF Vacuum RF Pump repair Lublin
Equipment Multipolar RF
Equipment type Aesthetic equipment
Fault Vacuum and RF
Location Lublin
SERVICE REQUEST

Weak suction and no RF heating

For this job, we travelled directly to a beauty studio in Lublin.

The customer reported two problems. The first was significantly reduced suction — the device drew weakly and could not generate the correct vacuum.

The second fault was a lack of heating from the RF element. One connection terminal was damaged, preventing proper contact in the circuit responsible for RF operation.

DIAGNOSTICS

We dismantled the device and checked the vacuum system

After removing the housing, we began diagnosing the components responsible for generating vacuum.

The problem was located in the pump. One of its components was cracked and leaking, causing vacuum loss and preventing the device from achieving the correct suction.

We also thoroughly inspected the hoses, connections and condition of the device interior.

VACUUM SYSTEM

Repairing and sealing the pump

After locating the leak, we repaired the damaged pump component and resealed it.

The entire system was then cleaned and checked again.

Resolving the leak restored correct vacuum generation and improved suction handpiece operation.

RF CONNECTION

A damaged pin prevented heating

The second fault concerned the connection between the RF element and the device.

One connector pin was damaged, preventing correct contact, and the RF element did not heat during operation.

The damaged connection was repaired. We soldered in a new contact, repaired the connector and checked connection stability.

WORK SCOPE

What did the service include?

01

Diagnostics

We dismantled the device and checked the vacuum system, pump and electrical connections.

02

Pump repair

We located a leak in the damaged pump component, repaired it and restored its seal.

03

RF repair

We repaired the damaged contact and connector connection responsible for RF element operation.

04

Cleaning

We cleaned the device interior and inspected its components before restarting it.

REPAIR RESULT

Vacuum and RF operation restored

After the repairs, the device was reassembled and checked.

Resolving the pump leak restored correct vacuum system operation.

Repairing the damaged contact and connector restored correct RF element operation.

All the work was performed at the customer's premises in Lublin, without transporting the complete device to our Wrocław service centre.
