## What Is LaserPoint and What Does This Device Do?

LaserPoint develops professional measuring instruments designed to assess the characteristics of laser radiation.

Depending on the sensor type and system configuration, such instruments can measure optical laser power or laser pulse energy.

Why is this important?

Because the numbers displayed on a cosmetic laser's control screen do not necessarily represent its actual optical output.

An operator may select a particular power setting, and the device may display that value correctly. However, this does not automatically confirm that the laser is delivering the expected optical power.

Verifying the actual output requires specialised measurement equipment and an appropriate measurement procedure.

These measurements provide valuable information when assessing the technical condition of laser systems.

## The Manufacturer Says 1200 W. But What Does the Laser Actually Deliver?

Cosmetic laser systems are frequently advertised with power ratings such as 800 W, 1200 W, 1600 W, or even higher.

However, these figures can describe different technical characteristics.

A manufacturer may refer to the nominal output of a diode module, the combined rating of several laser sources, electrical input power, or another specified parameter.

These figures should not automatically be interpreted as the optical power delivered at the treatment handpiece.

A proper technical assessment therefore begins with understanding exactly what the manufacturer specifies and under which operating conditions.

**The objective is not to achieve the highest possible power output. It is to verify that the device performs according to its intended technical specifications.**

## Why Is Measuring Actual Laser Output Important?

### 1. Treatment Effectiveness

Laser treatment effectiveness depends on the energy delivered to the target area.

If the device delivers less energy than expected under its selected operating conditions, treatment performance may be affected.

However, higher power does not automatically mean better treatment results.

Wavelength, pulse duration, spot size, cooling performance, and appropriate treatment settings are also essential factors.

### 2. Client Safety

Excessive energy delivery or unstable laser output may increase the risk of unwanted skin reactions.

Monitoring laser output characteristics is therefore an important part of responsible equipment maintenance and operation.

### 3. Identifying Hidden Technical Problems

A laser may start normally, display no error messages, and appear to function correctly while its actual output differs from the expected values.

Potential causes include problems with the laser diode module, power supply, electrical connections, optical components, or other parts of the system.

An optical measurement alone does not necessarily identify the defective component, but it provides useful evidence when combined with a comprehensive technical diagnosis.

### 4. Verification After Repairs

Replacing a damaged component does not always mean that a laser has fully recovered its intended performance.

Where appropriate, post-repair testing should include checking the device's operating parameters against the manufacturer's technical requirements.

Optical measurements can be an important part of this verification process.

## Are There Mandatory Laser Power Standards in Poland?

Poland and the European Union have regulations and technical standards addressing laser safety and performance.

However, **there is no single mandatory optical power rating, such as 800 W or 1200 W, that applies to every cosmetic laser.**

Relevant technical standards include:

- **PN-EN 60825-1** — safety of laser products, classification, and requirements.

- **PN-EN IEC 60601-2-22** — particular safety and essential performance requirements for certain medical and cosmetic laser equipment.

- **PN-EN ISO 11554:2025-11** — measurement methods for laser beam power, energy, and temporal characteristics.

Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/2346 also establishes common specifications for certain categories of aesthetic devices.

For professional equipment within the scope of the relevant provisions, manufacturer instructions must address measurements of light energy density and safety measures at least annually.

It is important to distinguish legally binding requirements from technical standards and the performance tolerances specified by individual equipment manufacturers.

## How Often Should Laser Equipment Be Checked?

The appropriate maintenance interval depends on the device model, operating conditions, manufacturer recommendations, and applicable regulatory requirements.

Regular technical inspections are particularly important for equipment used intensively in beauty salons and aesthetic clinics.

Cooling system contamination, component wear, damaged electrical connections, and optical problems can affect laser performance even when no error messages appear.
