What Water Should You Use in a Diode Laser Cooling System?

2026-11-21

Can you use ordinary tap water?

Many diode laser systems rely on liquid cooling to transfer heat away from sensitive components.

The quality of the cooling medium can influence equipment reliability.

Tap water contains dissolved minerals and other substances that vary according to the water supply.

Depending on the equipment, these substances may contribute to deposits, corrosion and restricted coolant circulation.

The correct coolant must always be selected according to the manufacturer's specifications.

Why boiling water is not enough

Boiling does not remove all dissolved mineral salts.

Likewise, household water filter jugs do not guarantee the quality required by professional laser cooling systems.

Neither method should be assumed to produce suitable coolant.

What is distilled water?

Distilled water is produced through evaporation and condensation.

This process removes a substantial proportion of dissolved inorganic impurities.

However, final water quality depends on production conditions, storage and potential subsequent contamination.

The label "distilled water" alone does not guarantee compatibility with every laser.

What is deionized water?

Deionized water is processed to remove dissolved ions.

This can result in very low electrical conductivity.

However, deionization does not automatically remove every organic compound, microorganism or suspended particle.

Additional purification requirements may apply depending on the equipment.

What does electrical conductivity mean?

Electrical conductivity describes how readily water conducts electric current.

It is commonly measured in microsiemens per centimeter, written as µS/cm.

Water containing fewer dissolved ions generally has lower conductivity.

Highly purified water may have conductivity significantly below 1 µS/cm, depending on temperature and measurement conditions.

There is no single conductivity limit suitable for every aesthetic laser.

Is lower conductivity always better?

Not necessarily.

Very low conductivity does not automatically mean that water is chemically compatible with every cooling system.

Material compatibility and corrosion behavior also matter.

Some systems require specific coolant formulations rather than simply the purest available water.

Can automotive antifreeze be used?

Automotive antifreeze, alcohol mixtures and other additives must not be introduced unless explicitly approved by the equipment manufacturer.

Certain formulations may be incompatible with tubing, seals or other components.

Some specialized cooling systems require formulated coolants, but the correct composition must be taken from the technical documentation.

How can water quality be measured?

An electrical conductivity meter, or EC meter, can be used to measure conductivity.

Many TDS meters estimate dissolved solids from conductivity rather than directly measuring every contaminant.

Measurements should account for temperature, instrument calibration and the manufacturer's acceptable limits.

How should cooling water be stored?

Store prepared cooling water in clean, tightly sealed containers made from suitable materials.

Avoid contamination from dust, dirty tools and repeated unnecessary opening.

Highly purified water can absorb carbon dioxide from the air, affecting conductivity.

There is no universal shelf life for every opened container of technical water.

A maintenance example from Wrocław

During preventive maintenance of an Aragon / Beauty Planet diode laser, Laser Tech Service technicians replaced the cooling filter and renewed the cooling water.

The visible condition of the used filter illustrated the importance of monitoring the cooling circuit.

Conclusion

Distilled and deionized water are produced using different purification methods.

Both can be appropriate for certain technical applications, but compatibility with a particular diode laser must be established from the manufacturer's requirements.
