Why Is Regular Water Filter Replacement Important in Diode Lasers?

The water filter in a diode laser is a small component that is easy to overlook. As long as the cooling system works properly, the condition of the filter and its connections may receive little attention.

Laser Tech ServicePublished: 28 October 2026

Emergency 808 nm laser service in Gliwice

Recently, Laser Tech Service received an urgent service request from Gliwice, Poland. A diode laser used for hair removal was overheating, and an on-site technician was needed.

During the inspection, our technician found a cracked fitting near the water filter, at the connection between the filter and the cooling hose. Coolant was leaking through the damaged connection.

The technician replaced the water filter and refilled the system with fresh coolant. Following the repair, the laser was working again.

Why do the filter and its connections need inspection?

Why does this matter? Water filters, plastic housings and fittings can deteriorate over time. Cracks may develop, causing coolant leaks. When the coolant level becomes too low, cooling performance can decrease and the laser may overheat.

When should you replace the filter and stop using the laser?

Regularly checking the water filter, cooling hoses and coolant level can help identify problems before they lead to equipment downtime. Filters should be replaced according to the manufacturer's instructions and actual operating conditions.

If your laser is overheating or losing coolant, stop using it until the cause has been identified and corrected.
