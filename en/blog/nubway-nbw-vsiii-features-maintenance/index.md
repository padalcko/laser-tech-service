## What is the NUBWAY NBW-VSIII?

The NUBWAY NBW-VSIII belongs to the category of vacuum roller massage and body contouring equipment.

Depending on the configuration, it combines vacuum suction, mechanical roller massage, radiofrequency energy and infrared technology.

The machine features a touchscreen control panel and interchangeable handpieces designed for different treatment areas.

This combination makes the system attractive to businesses seeking a versatile treatment platform.

## Technologies used in the NUBWAY NBW-VSIII

### Vacuum massage

The vacuum system creates a pressure difference that gently lifts tissue into the treatment handpiece.

The suction intensity can be adjusted according to the selected treatment and application area.

Proper operation depends on several components, including the vacuum pump, pneumatic tubing, connectors, seals and control valves.

### Mechanical roller massage

Selected handpieces combine vacuum suction with mechanical rollers.

The rollers provide mechanical tissue manipulation while the operator moves the handpiece across the skin.

The combination of rollers and adjustable vacuum allows different levels of massage intensity.

### Radiofrequency technology

Radiofrequency technology uses electromagnetic energy to generate controlled heating within tissue.

In aesthetic equipment, RF is commonly used in procedures intended to improve the appearance and firmness of the skin.

Actual outcomes depend on treatment settings, individual characteristics and correct equipment operation.

### Infrared technology

Infrared technology provides an additional source of thermal energy during certain treatments.

Combining IR with RF, vacuum suction and mechanical rollers expands the range of available treatment functions.

## Why do vacuum handpieces lose suction?

Reduced suction is a common technical problem in vacuum massage equipment.

The machine may power on normally and the touchscreen may respond correctly, while the handpieces fail to produce sufficient negative pressure.

Potential causes include:

- Contaminated pneumatic valves.

- Leaking connectors or tubing.

- Worn seals.

- Blocked vacuum passages.

- Vacuum pump malfunctions.

- A faulty vacuum release valve.

Insufficient suction does not automatically mean that the vacuum pump needs replacing.

A proper diagnosis should include inspection of the complete pneumatic circuit before expensive components are replaced.

## What does a vacuum release valve do?

A vacuum release valve, also called a vacuum vent valve, controls the release of negative pressure from the pneumatic system.

It allows pressure to equalize when required by the operating cycle.

If the valve becomes contaminated, sticks or loses its sealing capability, the system may experience unstable suction, pressure loss or incorrect vacuum release.

In some cases, the valve can be removed, cleaned and repaired.

Whether repair is appropriate depends on its design, condition and the nature of the malfunction.

## How to maintain the NUBWAY NBW-VSIII

Regular cleaning of the treatment handpieces, inspection of pneumatic tubing and compliance with the manufacturer's maintenance instructions can help reduce equipment failures.

Operators should pay attention to changes in suction strength, unusual pump noises, irregular roller operation and inconsistent vacuum adjustment.

If the equipment starts behaving abnormally, it is advisable to stop using it and arrange a professional inspection.

Disassembling pneumatic valves without appropriate technical knowledge may damage seals or create additional faults.
