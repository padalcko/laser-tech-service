## Złota Firma 2026 — what does it mean for Laser Tech Service?

Our award bears the inscription “Laureat Konkursu ZŁOTA FIRMA 2026” — winner of the Złota Firma 2026 competition. The information printed on it states that the distinction is based on existing ratings, credible online reviews and analysis of those data.

This approach is also described on the [official Złota Firma project website](https://www.zlotafirma.pl/how-it-works): publicly available customer reviews are the starting point. For us, what matters most is the people behind those ratings and their experience of working with our service team.

We see the award as welcome recognition that care and responsibility for entrusted equipment matter. It also motivates us to bring the same attention to the next device.

## Everyday work behind the award

Our daily work involves aesthetic lasers and equipment. We diagnose faults, repair electronics and power supplies, and work on cooling systems and handpieces. Diode laser servicing and repair are a significant part of this — from identifying why a device malfunctions to checking it after repair.

We work in Wrocław and serve clients from towns and cities across Poland. Our support is described on the [servicing page](https://lasertechservice.pl/en/services/), while individual devices and completed work appear in [our case studies](https://lasertechservice.pl/en/case-studies/). These everyday tasks, often unglamorous, form the story of Laser Tech Service.

## Laser and aesthetic equipment servicing means more than replacing parts

A device may start yet fail to perform its function. The symptom does not always identify the damaged component. Aesthetic equipment diagnostics therefore requires measurements, connection checks and investigation, including when a fault is unusual.

A good example is the [3000 NAIN laser power supply repair](https://lasertechservice.pl/en/case-studies/3000-nain-power-supply-repair/). Such cases underline the importance of understanding how the particular circuit works. For us, equipment repair begins with identifying the problem, not guessing which part to replace.

## Someone’s livelihood depends on every device

For a clinic owner, a breakdown means far more than a message on a screen. It means cancelled treatments, rescheduled appointments, calls to clients and downtime. The equipment stops, but the clinic’s commitments remain.

That is why we focus on finding the actual cause and returning the device to work. Aesthetic equipment servicing requires technical precision and an understanding that someone needs clear information about their equipment.

The award stays in the workshop, but our thanks go to you. Without clients and their trust, this story would not exist.
