Beauty Planet 3D Contour – what does it do, how does it work and is it worth having in a clinic?

Beauty Planet 3D Contour is an example of a multifunction device combining several body contouring technologies in one platform. For clients, this provides access to different treatments; for clinic owners, it offers scope to expand services without installing several separate devices.

14 September 2026 approximately 10 min read

What is Beauty Planet 3D Contour?

Beauty Planet 3D Contour is a multifunction aesthetic device designed primarily for body treatments and body contouring.

Its main advantage is not one particular technology, but the combination of several treatment methods in a single system.

In an earlier configuration of this model, the manufacturer presented 3D Contour as a 5-in-1 device.

The platform provides:

  • cryolipolysis,
  • ultrasonic liposuction,
  • endermomassage,
  • lipolaser,
  • radiofrequency (RF) combined with vacuum.

One machine can therefore support several treatment categories without the space required for multiple independent devices.

Cryolipolysis

One of the 3D Contour’s functions is cryolipolysis.

The system uses a dedicated handpiece with controlled cooling and vacuum.

According to the manufacturer’s specifications, the cryolipolysis operating temperature range for this version is -10°C to +5°C.

For a clinic, this allows procedures using controlled cooling to be offered without purchasing a separate device dedicated exclusively to that technology.

Endermomassage, vacuum and RF

Another important part of Beauty Planet 3D Contour is the handpiece combining vacuum, rollers and radiofrequency (RF).

The operator can adjust vacuum intensity, roller operation and RF settings directly through the device interface.

According to the manufacturer, the endermomassage system can operate at a vacuum of up to 100 kPa.

This was the part of the device involved in our recent service work in Wrocław.

Lipolaser

The 3D Contour also includes a set of lipolaser pads for different body areas.

In the configuration presented by the manufacturer, the device had 8 large and 4 small pads.

For the clinic, this provides another technology within the same treatment platform.

Ultrasonic liposuction

Ultrasound is another function of the device.

According to the manufacturer’s specifications, the ultrasound handpiece operates at approximately 27–40 kHz.

This further broadens the range of treatments available from a single device.

What does this type of device offer clinic clients?

From a client’s perspective, the main benefit is the ability to select a procedure for a particular objective and body area.

One client may use a vacuum and roller procedure, another cryolipolysis and another RF or lipolaser treatments.

A multifunction platform gives the clinic more flexibility in developing its services and selecting available technologies.

The particular procedure must, of course, be selected by an appropriately trained person in accordance with the device manufacturer’s instructions.

Why might 3D Contour be of business interest?

For a clinic owner, one of the device’s most important features is its versatility.

Instead of several separate devices, one platform with different treatment heads and handpieces can be used.

This is particularly relevant in smaller clinics, where every square metre matters.

One device also allows several different services to be listed and treatment packages involving different technologies to be developed.

This does not, of course, guarantee profitability.

Business results depend on factors including purchase price, client numbers, service pricing, running costs and how the clinic is managed.

Nevertheless, offering several treatment types from one platform may be an important consideration when planning an investment.

What happens when one function stops working?

Versatility also has another side.

Several separate systems operate within one device: electronics, power supply, vacuum, RF, cooling, cables, connectors and multiple handpiece types.

Failure of one function therefore does not necessarily mean the entire device is faulty.

The Beauty Planet 3D Contour unit we diagnosed in Wrocław is a good example.

An actual Beauty Planet 3D Contour repair in Wrocław

The problem concerned the handpiece responsible for functions including vacuum and RF.

During diagnostics, we established that the handpiece was not receiving power correctly.

As a result:

  • the vacuum system did not work,
  • the RF function did not work.

We diagnosed the device and handpiece, located the cause and carried out the repair.

After completing the work, we checked both repaired functions.

Not every fault requires transporting the entire device

This is particularly important for large aesthetic devices.

When one function stops working, the first response is often to arrange transport of the whole machine to a service centre.

This is not always necessary.

A large device may weigh several dozen kilograms, have numerous handpieces and require suitable protection for transport.

A better first step is therefore often on-site diagnostics.

A technician can establish whether the problem concerns the whole device, a particular handpiece, cable, connector, power supply or electronics.

Only once the cause is known can it be decided whether the repair is possible on site or a particular component or the whole device needs to go to the service centre.

Not every fault in a large device requires transporting the whole machine. Sometimes the first and best step is simply proper on-site diagnostics.

Diagnostics before transport can save time and costs

Transporting a large aesthetic device involves more than the delivery charge.

It also means protecting the equipment appropriately, arranging carriage and downtime during which the clinic cannot use it.

If the fault concerns one handpiece or a specific component, transporting the entire machine may be unnecessary.

For similar faults, contact the service team first, describe the symptoms carefully and agree on the best diagnostic approach.

Frequently asked questions

What is Beauty Planet 3D Contour?

It is a multifunction aesthetic device intended mainly for body treatments. It combines several technologies, including cryolipolysis, endermomassage, lipolaser, ultrasound and RF with vacuum, in one platform.

Does a handpiece fault mean the whole device has failed?

No. The problem may be confined to a particular handpiece, power supply, cable, connector or one of the device’s systems. Diagnostics is therefore worthwhile before arranging transport.

Does the device always need transporting to a service centre?

Not always. In some cases, diagnostics can be carried out at the client’s premises, with the next repair steps decided once the cause is established.
