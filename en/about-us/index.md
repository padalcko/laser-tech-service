## Our Story

Laser Tech Service was founded in 2024 on the belief that equipment servicing could be done differently.

The story of our company began with its founder, Dmytro Zaitsev, who has more than five years of hands-on experience repairing laser and aesthetic equipment.

While working in the service industry, he saw how important the approach to a problem was, not just the final repair result.

In some cases, replacing an entire assembly was preferred over investigating whether an individual component could be repaired. For customers, this could mean unnecessary additional costs.

That experience inspired him to establish a company built on a different approach.

Laser Tech Service was founded in 2024.

From the beginning, our goal has been to create a service company that identifies the cause of a malfunction before recommending a repair strategy.

We do not replace components simply because replacement is easier. We first assess whether repair or refurbishment is technically feasible, safe and economically reasonable.

Today, we help beauty salons, aesthetic clinics and other businesses maintain their equipment through workshop repairs in Wrocław and on-site service visits.

> We look for a solution first. We replace components only when necessary.

## How We Work

1. ### Contact

The customer describes the problem, and we collect essential information about the equipment and symptoms.
2. ### Diagnostics

We inspect the equipment, identify the cause of the failure and evaluate possible solutions.
3. ### Repair Approval

We explain the proposed work and conditions. Repairs begin after customer approval.
4. ### Repair and Testing

We repair or replace the necessary components and verify the equipment's operation.
5. ### Handover

We explain the work performed and the warranty conditions applicable to the specific repair.
