What can an inspection include?

  • Checking the device's technical condition.
  • Identifying signs of wear and potential faults.
  • Cleaning and servicing components appropriate to the device.
  • Checking the operation of key systems.
  • Checking the device after servicing.
  • Recommendations for continued use.

Important information and pricing questions

We agree the scope and cost before starting work. Below we explain what affects a quotation.

The price list gives indicative figures. The actual fault, equipment design, parts required and scope of work affect the final quotation, which we agree after diagnostics.

Diagnostics take place before the repair scope and final quotation are established. On-site diagnostics are approximately EUR 165 plus travel. This does not mean that diagnostics are automatically included in the repair price. For diode lasers, the terms on the diagnostics page apply: from EUR 165 without a repair order, or free when you commission Laser Tech Service to carry out the repair. Travel remains separately chargeable.

For diode replacement, EUR 330 covers labour only; the new diode is quoted separately. Parts for CO₂ and picosecond laser repairs are charged in addition. For other items, we confirm the parts scope and cost individually in the quotation.

Different models have different designs, components and parts availability. The equipment category alone does not establish the precise repair scope.

The indicative rate is EUR 1 per billable kilometre. We agree the distance and final cost before the visit. The calculator does not determine whether the distance covers a one-way or return journey.
